Scientific HPC learning environment

Learn. Build. Diagnose.
Transfer with confidence.

SAIL-HPC is a browser-first Slurm learning environment for practicing resource requests, job scripts, monitoring, diagnosis and revision before moving to a real cluster.

Deterministic training model

No credentials. No production Slurm. No real jobs. No hidden telemetry. Transfer guidance is always site-specific.
