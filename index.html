Research perspective

Microbial evolution,
examined through genomes.

I am Muhammad Bilal, a PhD researcher in the Battistuzzi Lab at Oakland University. I investigate how microbial genomes change, how those changes alter phylogenetic inference, and what they can reveal about evolutionary history across deep time.

My route into evolutionary genomics began in biotechnology and microbial genome analysis. Today, I work across population-scale pangenomics, phylogenomics, molecular clocks, high-performance computing, and astrobiology.

The connecting idea is simple: analytical choices should remain visible. I therefore pair biological questions with reproducible workflows, sensitivity analysis, and software that other researchers can inspect and reuse.

Open research ecosystem

One chain of evidence.
An evolving toolkit.

This collection grows with the research. Each project addresses a specific analytical need, while remaining compatible with a larger, transparent path from genome curation to evolutionary interpretation.

01

PanPhyloFlow

Genome → pangenome → phylogeny, without hiding the choices.

A teaching-oriented Nextflow workflow that connects annotation, Roary or Panaroo, threshold-aware summaries, IQ-TREE 3, and an inspectable report.

NextflowRoaryPanarooIQ-TREE 3
02

PanGenFlow

Turn an NCBI taxon query into a traceable genome cohort.

Genome retrieval, GCA/GCF reconciliation, metadata capture, explicit quality filters, and optional close-genome relatedness checks.

NCBI DatasetsCheckM2FastANIHPC
03

SpeciesResolve

Treat species delineation as evidence, not one cutoff.

Combines genome quality, ANI, alignment fraction, GTDB context, dereplication, and phylogenomics while preserving concordant, discordant, and unresolved cases.

ANIGTDB-TkdRepPhylogenomics
04

PanOrd

Read genome-level structure inside a gene-content matrix.

An interpretable ordination workbench using PCA, Jaccard PCoA, metadata-aware visualization, and candidate gene-cluster loadings.

RPCAJaccard PCoARoary
05

Openness Estimator

Measure how a pangenome grows as genomes are added.

Permutation-based accumulation curves and Heaps' law fitting with explicit gamma interpretation and publication-ready figures.

PythonHeaps' lawPermutationVisualization
06

PhyloClockLab

Learn → apply → ship molecular-clock analysis.

A learning-first laboratory for reproducible deep-time phylogenomics, beginning with manual understanding and sensitivity analysis before automation.

MCMCTreePAMLCalibrationSlurm
